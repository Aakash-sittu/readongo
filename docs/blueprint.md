# **App Name**: Vektor Insight

## Core Features:

- Live News Feed: Real-time consumption of cards via a precision-cached DB endpoint, displaying global tech insights instantly.
- AI Precision Summarizer: An AI-powered tool that transforms long-form headlines into punchy one-sentence insights to maximize information density.
- Category Intelligence: Advanced algorithmic sorting of articles into logic-driven sectors like AI/ML, Tech, and Product.
- Monolith Scroll Interface: A single-page, high-cohesion scrolling experience that eliminates navigation friction for news discovery.
- Atomic Card Layout: Information architecture using the International Typographic Style for maximum readability and structure.
- Search & Query Engine: Direct, ultra-fast querying of the article database with predictive auto-completion.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep Midnight (#293D9B) representing intellectual authority and industrial precision.
- Background: Clean Alabaster (#F7F7FA), heavily desaturated for an ultra-clean Swiss canvas.
- Accent: Arctic Blue (#1E9CCF) used for interactive call-to-actions and focused visual indicators.
- Primary and Body font: 'Inter' - a modern grotesque sans-serif chosen for its objective, machined, and neutral look that fits the grid system perfectly.
- A strict 12-column grid-based layout inspired by the International Typographic Style, ensuring a single cohesive scrolling narrative with massive whitespace and systematic order.
- Interactions should feel snappy and precise, featuring horizontal slide-ins and discrete card expansions that maintain the integrity of the layout.
- Geometric, weight-uniform icons that reflect Bauhaus principles, avoiding decoration in favor of clear semiotic function.
